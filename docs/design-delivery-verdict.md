## Where analysts disagreed, and my ruling

**1. Deck page mapping.** The brief's assumed uniform one-page shift is wrong. A text-sequence alignment shows old p27+p28 collapsed into new p27 (−1) and old p229 expanded into new p228–230 (+2), so new *n* maps to old *n+1* for n∈28..227 and old *n−1* for n∈231..241. **I accept this** — it is the only analysis that aligned the full text sequence, and it self-corrected its own p236 error rather than defending it. Consequence: **p124 and p236 are false positives** in the 16-changed-page list (8 and 36 antialiased pixels, max channel delta 12/255), so only 14 pages carry real edits.

**2. "16px/100%" counts: 138 vs 193.** Not a contradiction — different denominators. 138 is the count within the 9 desktop *page* frames; 193 is across all 31 spec files (adding legal, utility and the About alternates). **I verified 193 / 70 / 172 = 435** and use that.

The "501"/"502" frames.** One analyst treated them as a possible distinct error set; another proved them mislabels. **The mislabel reading is correct** — content is verbatim 404/500 copy, and the canvas offset (+2020px from the desktop twin) matches every one of the 11 other desktop→768 pairs with no exception.
