  benefits:
    - title: 'Nothing to puzzle over'
      body: 'When the next step is obvious, people stop hesitating, and stop writing to support to ask where things are or why something didn’t work.'
    - title: 'Cheaper to change'
      body: 'Moving a button on a design takes moments. Moving the same button once it’s built takes far longer, involves more people and costs more.'
    - title: 'One shared picture'
      body: 'Seeing real screens before the build means everyone agrees on what’s being made, rather than each person holding their own idea of it.'
    - title: 'Built around people'
      body: 'We look at how the people using it actually work, and design for what they need rather than what’s easiest to build.'
    - title: 'Consistent throughout'
      body: 'A shared set of styles and components keeps every screen consistent as the product grows, and makes each new screen quicker to build.'
    - title: 'Every screen size'
      body: 'Screens are designed for phones and large displays alike, rather than stretched from one size to fit the other and looking wrong on both.'
