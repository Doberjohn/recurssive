## Challenge

The app opened to a spinner for four seconds on the devices most of its
customers actually owned. Internal testing happened on flagship phones, so the
problem was invisible to the team that built it.

## Solution

We measured on the real device distribution first, which reframed the problem
from "the app is slow" to "startup is slow on 2 GB Android". From there the work
was specific: defer non-critical modules out of the startup path, move to Hermes
with a precompiled bytecode bundle, and split the bundle so the first screen no
longer waited on the whole app.

## Impact

Time to interactive fell from 4.1s to 1.5s at the 75th percentile. Crash-free
sessions rose as a side effect, because a large share of crashes were happening
during the long startup window.
